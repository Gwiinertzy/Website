About Me

Hello! I'm Marvin Borja, and I'm 18 years old. I'm a student taking information technology, and I'm also an artist who loves traditional and digital art. While I'm still studying, I spend most of my time creating personal works that help me develop my style and creativity. I have always liked making art that shows how I feel and what I think. I'm always learning, growing, and trying to become a better version of me with every work I create.

I grew up in a very poor household. Despite all the difficulties my parents had to go through, this contributed to my character formation. Despite the challenges we encountered growing up, we were always there for each other. From that experience, I realized the true value of family.

There were certain things that I regret in retrospect, but every bad experience has taught me something valuable, and I'm extremely happy for all of the things I've got now.
