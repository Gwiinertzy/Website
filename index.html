About Me

I'm a 19-year-old student from 12 ITEM 01, currently taking Bachelor of Science in Information Technology, major in Multimedia Arts and Animation at Universidad de Dagupan.

I am a passionate creative artist who enjoys expressing ideas through graphic design, photography, and visual storytelling. I have experience in creating publication materials (pubmats) for both school and church, as well as participating in various leadership roles and creative competitions.

Beyond my academic work, I love dancing and watching my favorite anime, which inspire my artistic style and imagination. I am continuously learning and improving my skills to become a better designer and multimedia artist.
