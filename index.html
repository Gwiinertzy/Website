About the Game

Overview

Classroom Clash is a 2D pixel-style action game that transforms the struggles of student life into an exciting battle for survival. What starts as an ordinary school year quickly turns into chaos as assignments come to life and deadlines become enemies.

The Story

Earyl James and Glinda Lia are just ordinary students trying to survive their final school year, but this year feels different.

Assignments keep piling up. Deadlines feel like enemies. Something isn't right.

One day, the classroom changes. The blackboard begins to glow, papers start floating, and suddenly school becomes a battlefield.

Now they must complete their tasks, fight their instructors, and overcome every challenge, or risk never graduating.

Game Mechanics

Health Bar

Reach 0 and it's game over. Takes damage from obstacles, lost tasks, and boss hits.

Task Bar

Collect 7 tasks to fill the bar and summon the boss. Losing tasks costs HP and progress.

Coffee Power-Up

Grants faster movement, attacks, and stronger damage for 5 seconds.

Boss HP

Deplete the boss's health to clear the level. Clear all three to graduate.
